📝 Resolução Teórica:
• Em t₁ = 1,0 s: x(1,0) = 20 + 5(1,0)² = 25,0 m
• Em t₂ = 2,0 s: x(2,0) = 20 + 5(2,0)² = 40,0 m
• Vel. Média (1s a 2s): v_méd = Δx/Δt = 15/1 = 15,0 m/s
• Vel. Instantânea em t₁=1s: v(t) = dx/dt = 10t = 10,0 m/s.
